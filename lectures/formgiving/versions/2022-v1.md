# Formgiving, version 1 (November 2022), outline

An early, short version of the lecture. About a dozen slides, partly in Czech, mostly titles. It
is kept here only as a record; the current deck is `../deck.md`.

1. **Formgiving**, title slide.
2. **Giving form to the future**: a quote on the Danish word for design.
3. **Expectation vs reality**: problem or idea on one side, solution or product on the other.
4. **Design process to the rescue**.
5. **Double diamond**: a diagram slide (picture only).
6. **Design sprint**, two slides (pictures only).
7. **Testing** (Czech): raises the chance of success, saves time and resources, is repeated and
   done as early as possible, aims to find errors and unclear places, typically with 5 testers.
8. **Research**, **Design**, **Validation** as three phase headings.
9. **Prototyping tools** (Czech list): Figma, Adobe XD, paper and stationery, LEGO, 3D printing,
   Wizard of Oz, wireframes.
10. **The role of design**, closing heading.
