# Storyboard

*Adapted from the Design Sprint method (Knapp, Zeratsky, Kowitz). Own adaptation for teaching.*

**Day:** Wednesday (Decision) | **Time needed:** 2-3 h

Use a storyboard to plan the prototype.

## Objectives (Why?)

Give the team a clear plan for building the prototype.

## Instructions (How?)

### Before starting

- If the team is not comfortable with sketching, run a short "everyone can sketch" warm-up.
- You may reuse parts of the earlier sketches.
- Make sure there is enough wall space.
- Share the work so everyone knows their part.

### The activity

- Divide a flipchart sheet into 15 frames.
- Fill the frames with the steps of the user's interaction with your solution.

### When finished

- Look for missing parts of the flow.
- Mark unclear parts that still need decisions.
- Settle the open questions by filling in or clarifying the storyboard.

## Expected outcome (What?)

A flipchart or whiteboard with a story of about 15 frames showing how users will work with your solution.

## Key ideas

1. Make it clear and easy to follow while prototyping.
2. Do not decide on the fly.
