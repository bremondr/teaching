# Ask the experts

*Adapted from the Design Sprint method (Knapp, Zeratsky, Kowitz). Own adaptation for teaching.*

**Day:** Monday (Understand) | **Time needed:** 20 min per expert

A set of short interviews with experts from inside the organisation and from outside, invited just for this session, to fill gaps in what the team knows.

## Objectives (Why?)

1. Collect relevant, specific information.
2. Confirm or disprove assumptions.
3. Change perspectives.
4. Fill gaps in understanding.

## Instructions (How?)

### Before starting

- Write down your assumptions.
- Write down your questions.
- Arrange for the experts to have time for the interview.

### The activity

- Spend about 20 minutes (more if needed) with each expert.
- Talk through your understanding of the challenge; use the map if it helps.
- Ask your questions and let them describe the problem and the solutions already tried.
- Take notes.

### When finished

Organise the notes and turn them into "How might we" questions (see [How might we questions](01-understand-how-might-we-questions.md)).

## Expected outcome (What?)

New information, "aha" moments, filled gaps, confirmed or disproved assumptions.

## Key ideas

1. Listen.
2. Do not lead.
3. Do not interrupt.
4. Do not judge.
5. Probe further when an answer is unclear.
