# How might we questions (HMWs)

*Adapted from the Design Sprint method (Knapp, Zeratsky, Kowitz). Own adaptation for teaching.*

**Day:** Monday (Understand) | **Time needed:** 30 min

Short questions built from earlier insights. They are prompts for ideation.

## Objectives (Why?)

1. Turn problems into opportunities.
2. Move attention from the current state to the future state.
3. Open the team to new solutions.

## Instructions (How?)

Everyone writes notes alone. When all are done, stick them on the wall while reading them aloud.

### The activity

1. Write "HMW" in the corner of each sticky note.
2. Write questions from your earlier insights. Ways to start: challenge the status quo, explore the opposite, challenge an assumption (first ask what the assumptions are).
3. One question per sticky note. A good HMW is:
   - not too broad, narrow enough to set boundaries,
   - open to a wide range of solutions,
   - about people,
   - positive and inspiring.

### When finished

1. Organise the notes.
2. Place them on the map.
3. Pick the most interesting ones by dot voting.

## Expected outcome (What?)

A set of questions that prompt ideation.
