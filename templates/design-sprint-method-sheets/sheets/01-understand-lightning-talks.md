# Lightning talks

*Adapted from the Design Sprint method (Knapp, Zeratsky, Kowitz). Own adaptation for teaching.*

**Day:** Monday (Understand) | **Time needed:** 15 min

Every member of the sprint team gives a very short talk about the challenge.

## Objectives (Why?)

1. Knowledge is spread unevenly in the team, so share it.
2. Gain new perspectives by listening to everyone.
3. Learn what the team knows and what it does not know yet.

## Instructions (How?)

Go round the team, starting with the decider. The listeners capture insights in a Rose-Thorn-Bud grid (rose: what works, thorn: a problem, bud: an opportunity).

### Before starting

Nothing special to prepare.

### The activity

- Each team member gives their view of the project or challenge in 1 minute.
- The rest of the team writes insights on sticky notes and sorts them into rose, thorn and bud.
- Keep all perspectives visible to build one overview of the challenge.

### When finished

Leave the notes on the wall. They are used again in the [4 step sketch](02-ideate-4-step-sketch.md).

## Expected outcome (What?)

A shared understanding of the challenge.
